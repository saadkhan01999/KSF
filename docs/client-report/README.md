## How the screenshots were made

Version 2.0 ran on this computer against a fresh MongoDB database. Four sample
pharmacies (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Peshawar) were created through the
normal sign-up, then given medicines, suppliers, purchase orders, staff,
customers, prescriptions, expenses, support requests and about 1,750 sales
through the API, exactly as the screens would. So that the charts show a
month, the dates of those sample sales were spread over the previous 30 days.
The Super Admin recorded the plan payments and answered two of the support
requests.

The screens were captured with headless Chrome at 1440 × 900 (laptop),
1920 × 1080 (wide monitor), 820 × 1180 (tablet) and 390 × 844 (phone).

The version 1.0.6 screenshots are from the previous round (2 October 2026),
taken with its demonstration pharmacy.
